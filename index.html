Scrap

We buy and collect PET bottles, heavy plastic, cartons, aluminium cans and nylon from households, traders and collection points across Akwa Ibom, sort it in our yard and sell it to processors.

  • PET bottles
  • Heavy plastic
  • Cartons
  • Aluminium cans
  • Nylon

Palm

We lease existing plantations, harvest the fruit, have it milled by a third party and sell palm oil and palm kernel.

  • Palm oil
  • Palm kernel
